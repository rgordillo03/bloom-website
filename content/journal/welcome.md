I'm Rafael. I'm building Bloom from my apartment in Cologne. This is the first journal entry — partly to introduce myself, partly so I have something to look back on later when this either works or doesn't.

## What Bloom is, in one sentence

A bamboo card on your fridge that opens a small app with one daily question. You write a few sentences. Something visual grows in your garden over time. That's it.

## Why I'm building it

I struggle with habits like most people do. Over the years I tried a lot of mindfulness apps. Most of them I forgot about. The ones I didn't forget about overwhelmed me with features, push notifications, and that low-grade pressure of "you've been doing well, don't break your streak."

What I actually wanted was something boringly small. One question. One quiet space. Show up when I show up. Don't punish me when I don't.

So I made it.

## What it isn't

It isn't a productivity tool. It isn't therapy. There are no streaks. There are no leaderboards. There's nothing to optimize. If you reflect 30 days in a row your garden grows. If you reflect 4 times in a month your garden grows too — just slower. That's fine.

## Where it is right now

Honestly? Still being built. The app works. The card works. I'm testing it with friends and with myself. Founding 100 members will be the first to receive a card and help me figure out what should change before a wider launch.

Thanks for being here this early. It means a lot.
